# GLOSSARY

## Public Intelligence

An open engineering initiative building a globally distributed, community-owned AI infrastructure.

---

## Node

A participant in the network that contributes compute resources and executes inference workloads.

---

## Scheduler

The central coordination system responsible for assigning workloads to suitable nodes based on available resources, health, latency, and geographic considerations.

---

## Heartbeat

A periodic message sent by a node indicating that it is alive and reporting its current health and resource status.

---

## Health Check

The process of determining whether a node is operating correctly and is capable of receiving workloads.

---

## Distributed Inference

Executing AI model inference across a distributed network rather than a single machine.

---

## Geographic Scheduling

Scheduling decisions that consider physical location, latency, regional availability, and network conditions.

---

## Community Node

A node operated by an individual or organization contributing compute resources to Public Intelligence.

---

## Inference Request

A request submitted by a user or application asking the network to execute an AI model.

---

## Resource Advertisement

Information sent by a node describing its available compute resources, such as CPU, GPU, memory, storage, bandwidth, and current utilization.

---

## AI Context

The organization-level documentation that serves as the authoritative source of truth for both human contributors and AI agents.
